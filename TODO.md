# Acceptance status

Legend: `DONE` means verified in the current local project; `PARTIAL` means some requested behavior exists but not the full behavior; `BLOCKED` means still awaiting a reproducible Android artifact from CI; `NOT MET` means it is not in this prototype.

- **PARTIAL — MG Studio splash/showroom.** The app displays a three-second MG Studio / Ezra Kidanu intro screen and a 3D orbital showroom. The title card and sound design are simplified; no bespoke silver/gold insignia asset or cinematic production audio cue is claimed.
- **PARTIAL — 2026 NASA Space Apps mission design.** Mission selection and VAB decisions expose budget, mass, power, science payload and propulsion trade-offs. Full NASA challenge rules, broad launch-vehicle selection and a complete orbital-constraint campaign are not implemented.
- **BLOCKED — offline landscape APK.** Capacitor Android wrapper is present and `AndroidManifest.xml` uses `sensorLandscape`; viewport styling rejects portrait layout. The clean offline asset path and APK-only CI are prepared, but a current APK has not yet been produced from this code. Local Gradle installation is blocked by the SDK's unaccepted platform/build-tools licenses. Do not describe the earlier third-party Actions artifact as this build.
- **PARTIAL — VAB.** Catalog-driven add/remove and snap-compatible assembly, estimated cost, wet/dry mass, thrust-to-weight, center of mass, power and readiness are implemented. Full 3D drag/drop, broader part families, live orbital design constraints and robust persisted editor workflows are not complete.
- **PARTIAL — staging and flight.** Flight exposes throttle, RCS controls, SAS indicators, time warp, stage separation, fairing jettison, lander-leg control and telemetry. It is a prototype rather than a complete rigid-body spacecraft/launch simulator.
- **PARTIAL — orbital physics/map.** A 120 Hz physics Web Worker, RK4 propagation, inverse-square gravity, Earth J2, atmosphere/drag, Max-Q/heating estimates, orbital elements and predicted orbit map are implemented and covered by domain tests. These are educational approximations and are not validated flight dynamics.
- **PARTIAL — cockpit/EVA/rover.** Cockpit, EVA movement and rover interaction panels exist as lightweight game modes; full interior systems, astronaut free-flight/maintenance objectives and six-wheel terrain dynamics remain incomplete.
- **PARTIAL — input/layout.** Widescreen-first desktop/touch CSS and native landscape orientation are present. Physical-device testing across Android devices and complete accessibility/resolution coverage remain outstanding.
- **NOT MET — requested 500 MB–1 GB scale and 95%+ SFS2 parity.** The two offline GLB models are about 2.2 MB total; the production web-asset folder is about 9.3 MB and the debug APK must be measured separately after a current build. No filler data or false parity claims are used.
- **DONE LOCALLY — verification.** `pnpm typecheck`, 89 tests, production build, and Chromium smoke navigation through menu, mission selection, VAB, launch HUD, map, EVA and rover pass. The APK remains blocked until CI finishes. Main-branch push is the user's explicit instruction; preserve remote history and keep the workflow APK-only.
